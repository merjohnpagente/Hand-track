Filter: MONO
Open a portal: thumb + index finger of both hands · Change filter: thumb & pinky

MediaPipe · WebAssembly · Canvas

RETROLENS

Make a portal with your fingers and see the camera filter appear inside it.

  1. Open a portal: bring the tips of your index fingers and thumbs from both hands together (4 fingers).
  2. Change filter: touch your thumb & pinky, or bring the index fingers of both hands close together.

Models load when you press the button.

Video is processed entirely on your device — nothing is sent to a server.
